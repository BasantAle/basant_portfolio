About

I am a mechanical engineer with a background in simulation-driven design, sustainable transportation systems, product fabrication, and engineering research.

My graduate work at Purdue University focused on lightweight body-in-white design through structural optimization, material substitution strategies, and finite element analysis — examining the tradeoffs between structural performance and energy-emission impact.

My experience spans industry, research, entrepreneurship, and public-sector engineering: DER compliance testing in solar energy, electric vehicle conversion research, vehicle inspection systems, and community-scale product deployment.

I work with SolidWorks, AutoCAD, ANSYS, COMSOL, CATIA, MATLAB, and Python, with a strong foundation in testing, troubleshooting, and applied design evaluation.

Featured Projects
01

Lightweight Body-in-White Structural Optimization

Purdue University · Master's Thesis

Investigated structural performance and environmental impact of lightweight vehicle design by integrating structural optimization and material substitution strategies in a BIW model.

  • Performed stiffness-based weight optimization of BIW using FEA in ANSYS; achieving 13-16% vehicle weight reduction.
  • Developed a lightweight vehicle model in MATLAB and simulated in a real driving condition; resulting in 4-5% reduction in fuel consumption & CO2 emission and 6-8% reduction in NOx emission.

Feasibility Study of Electric Safari Vehicle

Youth Scientist Encouragement Fund

Coordinated a project to implement electric conversion technology in safari vehicles in a national park, for cleaner transportation in an environmentally sensitive setting.

Developed a functional e-scooter prototype as a part of a project to study the feasibility of e-scooter manufacturing in Lumbini Province, Nepal.
